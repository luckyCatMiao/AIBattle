**What went wrong in round 2** (both games lost by base destruction)
- **r2-g1 (I played east), lost at t=219:** I lost the early skirmish at t=30–49, and by t=76 it was my 3 tanks against sol's 5. At t=77–92 my retreat used `move`, so my tanks didn't fire back while sol chased them. I lost 4 tanks and sol lost none.
- **r2-g2 (I played west), lost at t=170:** I lost the same early fight at t=30–60 and was outnumbered in tanks from then on.
- **How sol fights:** I ran a script over sol's commands. All 142 of its combat-unit `move` orders came within 260 px of my units, typically just after a shot: it fires, steps back about 45 px while reloading, then returns. It also mostly attacks my nearest tank from the edge of range, so several of its tanks hit one of mine while my others can't reach. My tanks just held position and traded shots, which loses against this.

**What changed in v3** (the economy and army movement are the same as v2)
- **Ready tanks** fire together at the weakest enemy within reach.
- **Reloading tanks** near an enemy step about 45 px back, leaning toward home. They don't do this close to my own base.
- **Retreats** now fire back on the way out instead of just running.
